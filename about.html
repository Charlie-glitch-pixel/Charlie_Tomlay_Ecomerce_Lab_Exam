About us

Old folks slippers are slippers that commonly used during 1990's to 2015 known for their simple design, comfort, and durability. They are commonly made from materials such as hard rubber and leather making them suitable for everyday use at home or outdoors. These slippers reflect the styles and craftsmanship of the past while providing durability and convenience. Even with the development of modern footwear, old folks slippers remain popular because of their affordability, simplicity, and timeless design.

Goal

The product goal is to promotes the revival of legacy by bringing back old-fashioned slipper designs and preserving the craftsmanship, and memories associated with them. By reviving these classic designs, the product seeks to preserve the beauty and simplicity of traditional footwear while improving its comfort, durability, and safety for modern users. This also allows younger generations to appreciate the value of traditional products and keep their legacy alive for the future.
